# ADR-ADR-0003: Definition of a GSPL seed

**Status:** Accepted (Prompt 1 baseline)
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Decision-maker:** GSPL Canon foundation

## Context

Multiple reference repos recover the seed as genome-shaped with , , , genes.

## Competing Options

(1) Genome-only. (2) Genome + optional metadata. (3) Open-ended.

## Evidence

Spec/01 enumerates required and optional fields. Canonical form in tests.

## Decision

GSPL seed is shape (2), required fields are , , , genes.

## Consequences

Hash material exclusion is mechanical and tested. Gene names may not begin with $.

## Risks

Drift in canonicalization between implementations breaks determinism.
