# ADR-ADR-0008: Compression claim boundaries

**Status:** Accepted (Prompt 1 baseline)
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Decision-maker:** GSPL Canon foundation

## Context

Universal-compression claims appear in early reports but are mathematically unsound.

## Competing Options

(1) Universal compression. (2) 8 rebuild classes (RC-1..RC-8). (3) Kolmogorov-bound.

## Evidence

Kolmogorov complexity of random data. RC-1..RC-8 enumerate defensible classes.

## Decision

Option (2) + (3). GSPL is bounded by Kolmogorov complexity.

## Consequences

COMPRESSION_BOUNDARIES.md is canonical. Claim GSPL-CLAIM-0004 is refuted.

## Risks

Misreading as universal-compression.

## Unresolved Questions

Per-class formal ratio bounds.
