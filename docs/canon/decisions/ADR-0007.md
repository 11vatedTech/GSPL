# ADR-ADR-0007: Translation fidelity model

**Status:** Accepted (Prompt 1 baseline)
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Decision-maker:** GSPL Canon foundation

## Context

Universal perfect translation is impossible. Bridges must declare losses.

## Competing Options

(1) Binary preserved/not-preserved. (2) Graduated fidelity levels. (3) LOSSES spec only.

## Evidence

TRANSLATION_BRIDGE_MODEL.md enumerates seven graded fidelity levels.

## Decision

Option (2). Every translation declares its fidelity level and a losses field.

## Consequences

Translation bridges become auditable end-to-end.

## Risks

Authors under-report fidelity.

## Unresolved Questions

Translation benchmark surface for fairness.
