# ADR-ADR-0005: Canonical representation candidates

**Status:** Accepted (Prompt 1 baseline)
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Decision-maker:** GSPL Canon foundation

## Context

Three candidate canonical seeds (genome, sem-graph, gen-program) were evaluated.

## Competing Options

(1) Genome only. (2) Semantic-graph only. (3) Generative-program only. (4) Hybrid. (5) Layered.

## Evidence

Comparative scoring in seed_model_candidates.md. Hostile review notes B alone loses deterministic hashing.

## Decision

Layered model: external form is the Genome; internal kernel traverses as a typed semantic graph.

## Consequences

Hash material is over the Genome form. Translation bridges operate on the Genome.

## Risks

Bloating the internal representation family eats into R&D bandwidth.

## Unresolved Questions

Subsumption proof and graph canonicalization.
